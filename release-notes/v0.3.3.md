The panel closes again after the Omarchy 4.0.3 update, and the RGB tab is rebuilt.

## Fixed
- The panel could be opened but never closed, which looked like a frozen machine because the
  panel holds exclusive keyboard focus. Omarchy 4.0.3 made `bar.centerHoverRevealSuppressed`
  read only and replaced it with a setter function, and `close()` assigned to it on its first
  line, so the exception aborted the function before it ever hid the panel. It now prefers the
  setter, cannot throw, and hides before anything else runs
- The bar icon turned into a fan glyph and greyed out whenever both fans reached 0 rpm, which
  happens normally on battery. Idle fans are not a fault, so the alien stays and the icon is no
  longer dimmed. A heat warning still gets its own glyph, and stale or missing readings still dim
- The battery and mute colours were painted and then immediately overwritten. Both restore paths
  built their commands inside Model.js and so bypassed the overlays entirely
- The RGB tab jumped to Chassis on open. Keyboard presence was defined as the device being
  present AND the last command having succeeded, so any transient error, including the new
  `hidraw-busy`, hid the keyboard surface

## Changed
- The chassis is drawn as the rear of the machine, the lid with its logo above a stadium ring
  around the io, rather than as a circle that resembled nothing on the laptop
- The power button moved to the keyboard surface, where it physically sits, above the end key
- The power button follows the battery: red below ten percent, green while charging, amber on
  battery. It is an overlay and is never written into the saved colours
- The colour wheel is hidden until the palette button next to the hex field is pressed, and the
  duplicate swatch and hex readout inside the picker are gone
- The panel is wider
- `close` and `hide` are exposed over IPC, so a wedged panel is always recoverable with
  `omarchy-shell alienware close`
