Lighting restores correctly after a cold boot.

## Fixed
- Saved lights restored in the wrong order. Brightness and colour were sent before the effect, and
  setting an effect resets the controller colour, so the saved colour was overwritten every time
- Colour is only sent for effects that accept a per LED colour. `alienwarectl rgb status` now
  reports `colorModes` from the controller mode flags, and the restore skips the colour step for
  effects like Spectrum Cycle and Rainbow Wave that drive their own palette
- Restore no longer gives up while openrgb is still starting. The retry backs off to 30 seconds
  after five attempts and keeps trying for 30, instead of 10 attempts at a fixed 3 seconds
- A controller that answers with zero zones no longer burns the one restore attempt

## Changed
- `restoreSavedLights` builds its command sequence through `Model.restoreSequence`, which is
  covered by tests instead of only being reachable from the running shell
