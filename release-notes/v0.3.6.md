A panel rework, a bar widget you can compose, and lighting that stays off.

## Fixed
- A cold AW-ELC controller is primed before an all-zero status is treated as wedged, so the RGB tab
  no longer reports the controller as stuck after a resume
- Lights switched off came back on their own: the battery colour on the power button, the mute
  indicator and a theme change all repainted without checking. Resume lit them again from firmware.
  Every repaint now respects the off state, and off is re-sent once the controller answers
- The power button could never take a colour of its own, because the battery overlay always won it
- A saved bar widget setting only took effect after a shell restart. The widget now reads its own
  settings, which update as soon as they are saved
- `nvidia-smi` ran on every poll and woke the discrete GPU, keeping it powered on battery. The
  helper checks `runtime_status` first and reports the GPU as asleep instead

## Added
- Battery on the power button is a switch on the Lighting tab, saved with the rest of the state.
  With it off, the power key is selectable on the keyboard map and takes any colour
- The bar widget takes any set of readings, each with its own icon: alien logo, thermal mode, CPU
  temperature, GPU temperature, fan speed and GPU power. Settings shows a live preview

## Changed
- The thermal mode sits once above the tabs instead of appearing on both the Fans and Power pages
- Sections are cards, the Power tab leads with stat tiles, and the tab strip is one control
- The keyboard is drawn like the hardware: dark caps with each legend lit in its own colour
- The colour picker is one card: wheel, brightness, preview, hex field, presets, theme swatches and
  an Apply button that names what it will paint
- Follow the Omarchy theme lives on the Lighting tab only, not in settings as well
